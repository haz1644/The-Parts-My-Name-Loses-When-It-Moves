# What your subject is, and in what way it is disappearing.

The subject is my name, in twenty forms it has. It changes with the person and the situation, it fades and born again, it has different shapes and form, but it is still my name.

# What the twenty records have in common — your heading, and the argument inside it.

The Title of the website "The Parts My Name Loses When It Moves" is the argument. All twenty records are the parts my name loses when it moves from one person to another, from the full name to a nickname, a typo or a number... Even my full name fades when time and memories passed. The twenty records are twenty encounters with that same name.

For "date", the blocked dates are lost days that I cannot remember, as they have no digital archive of when they reached me, I cannot trace anything other than their handwriting and the name.

For "not_captured", every line begins with a person: My, Her, His, Their, Our... Someone's feeling or action is hard to be captured.

For "certainty", a 5 is the full name I can prove, and a 1 means names that I cannot recognize.
